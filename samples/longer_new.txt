Rain tapped softly against the window. Daniel set his cup beside the open book and turned another page. Outside, the town was waking up. A delivery van stopped near the bakery, and someone called a greeting across the street. He had planned to leave an hour ago, but the letter on the table had changed everything. He read the final line once more, folded the paper carefully, and reached for his coat. This time, he knew exactly where he needed to go.
