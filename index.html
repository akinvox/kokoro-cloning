About this project

I’m building this for my audiobook site, akinvox.com . The goal is voice cloning without sacrificing quality or speed.

V1 has had 100k cloning updates on pretrained Kokoro. I believe more training could improve it substantially, and work on v2 is ongoing.

The site is still under construction. I’d appreciate you giving it a go.
